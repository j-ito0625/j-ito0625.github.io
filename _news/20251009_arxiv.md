We are pleased to announce that our latest research paper, **"Data-Driven Adaptive PID Control Based on Physics-Informed Neural Networks"** by Junsei Ito and Yasuaki Wasa, has been published on [arXiv](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.04591).

This work proposes a data-driven PID controller design utilizing adaptive gain optimization through Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs). By leveraging automatic differentiation of PINNs, the method enables model predictive control using a cost function based on tracking error and control inputs. The approach achieves adaptive gain tuning for stability and system nonlinearities, providing a systematic framework for integrating PINNs-based models into closed-loop control systems. Numerical experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method in both time and frequency domains.

**Comments:** This work has been submitted to the IEEE Transactions on Control Systems Technology for possible publication.  
**Subjects:** Systems and Control (eess.SY); Machine Learning (cs.LG)
